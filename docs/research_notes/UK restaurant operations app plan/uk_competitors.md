# UK Competitive Landscape: Restaurant/Hospitality Ops, Compliance & Checklist Software (research as of 6 Oct 2026)

Note on sources: several price comparisons come from blog posts written by competing vendors (Hubl, Lemon). Those are third-party figures with a commercial bias and are flagged "[3P]". Vendor-page figures are flagged "[Vendor]". Aggregator figures (Capterra/G2/GetApp) are flagged "[Agg]" and are often stale or use the wrong unit (per user vs per site).

## Q1. Check65 / Alert65 (Food Alert, part of Citation Group): features, pricing, bundles, customers, reviews

### Takeaway
Alert65 is a consultancy-led compliance platform (HACCP, risk assessments, policies, checklists, allergens, training, incidents). Check65 is its mobile "due diligence" app for checklists and temperature checks. No price is published. The software is sold inside Independent/Multisite/Enterprise packages alongside memberships, audits, consultancy and a 24/7 advice line. Food Alert belongs to Citation Group, which also sells HR, H&S and ISO services, so the bundle and cross-sell threat is the main thing that sets it apart.

### Cited Findings
- Check65 features: turns paper checklists into tasks with real-time status, mirrors org structure (regions, sites, departments, users), records deliveries, hot/cold food and visitor logs, logs corrective actions, supports international time zones, and has dashboards that work with BI tools. "Bluetooth Temperature Checking" for automated fridge/freezer monitoring is sold as a separate, related offer. No pricing is shown on the page — [Food Alert: Due diligence in the palm of your hand](https://www.foodalert.com/due-diligence-in-the-palm-of-your-hand/)
- The App Store listing calls Check65 the "due diligence" companion to the Alert65 food hygiene and H&S platform. It covers daily/weekly/monthly scheduled checklists (opening/closing, cleaning, fire safety), ad-hoc checklists (visitor logs), hot checks (cooking, reheating, hot hold), cold checks (cooling, ambient, fridge, freezer) and delivery checks. Used in hospitality, leisure, education and healthcare — [App Store Check65](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1611955849). (The UK App Store page returned HTTP 429, so the rating and review count were not captured.)
- Alert65 modules: digital HACCP with 200+ templates, 200+ consultant-written risk assessments with version history, group-wide safety policies that sites can customise, daily checklists and temperatures, allergen matrix and menu compliance, training tracking, certificate expiry reminders, RAG-scored task accountability, food complaints and incidents, and EHO visit preparation. Works offline and is ISO 27001 certified — [Food Alert Alert65](https://www.foodalert.com/alert65)
- Packaging: tiered Independent / Multisite / Enterprise packages. Optional extras include compliance membership, audits, consultancy, the 24/7 advice line, HACCP development, training/e-learning, SALSA/BRCGS supply-chain work and allergen/nutrition analysis. The page says it is "suitable for all business sizes and budgets" but gives no prices — [Alert65](https://www.foodalert.com/alert65)
- Customers: "3,000+ businesses", named as Fuller's, Heavenly Desserts, Wasabi and Lunya — [Alert65](https://www.foodalert.com/alert65)
- Ownership: The Citation Group acquired Food Alert in 2019. Since then Food Alert has bought SureFoot Solutions, NT Assure, STS and Common Sense Compliance (four acquisitions in about 18 months, around 2023) — [Food Alert news](https://www.foodalert.com/food-alert-steps-up-growth-with-latest-acquisition/); [Catering Today](https://www.cateringtoday.co.uk/news/regulations-policies/food-alert-acquires-surefoot-solutions-ltd); [Food Alert: Common Sense Compliance](https://www.foodalert.com/food-alert-acquires-common-sense-compliance-ltd/). Food Alert's site also points to the Citation Group modern slavery statement — [Food Alert](https://www.foodalert.com/due-diligence-in-the-palm-of-your-hand/)
- Food Alert runs PPC landing pages for the checklist app and for software ("Streamline your compliance checks & to do lists"), which suggests it is actively acquiring customers for the app on its own — [Food Alert PPC](https://www.foodalert.com/checklists-app-ppc-v2/); [Software PPC](https://www.foodalert.com/software-ppc-v2/)
- A competitor comparison lists Alert65 as "custom quote" with "consultant-supported implementation" [3P] — [Lemon blog, checked 26 Sep 2026](https://www.getlemon.app/resources/blog/best-food-safety-apps-uk-kitchens-2026)
- Cross-channel link: Trail sells a "Trail + Surefoot" bundle at £105/site/month that includes expert safety guidance. Surefoot is a Food Alert/Citation company [Vendor/search snippet] — [Trail x Surefoot](https://trailapp.com/surefoot)

### Inferences
- Citation's model is retainer-led: H&S, HR and employment-law advice under multi-year contracts, plus Food Alert's food safety services. Alert65/Check65 is probably sold mainly as a sweetener or bundle inside consultancy retainers rather than as standalone SaaS. Expect pricing that is opaque, quote-based and multi-year.
- A new app that combines checklists, food safety, R&M and handbooks overlaps with the whole Citation bundle (Food Alert plus Citation HR/H&S). Self-serve, transparent pricing and month-to-month terms would set it clearly apart.

### Gaps
- No published Alert65/Check65 price, even as a hint. No App Store UK or Trustpilot rating captured (429 error). The Citation Atlas platform and any cross-sell were not checked. No date found for the "palm of your hand" page.

## Q2. Profiles of UK players: features, segment, pricing, terms, hardware, ownership

### Takeaway
The market splits into tiers. (a) Cheap self-serve SFBB/temperature apps at £5–£32/site/month (SFBB+, SFBB Pro, Hubl, Lemon, food-safety.app, Leafe). (b) Mid-market ops/checklist SaaS at £32–£83/site/month with 12-month minimums (Trail by Access Group, Paddl, FoodDocs). (c) Hardware-plus-software sensor vendors on 12–24 month contracts (Navitas, Checkit). (d) Consultancy-bundled platforms with quote-only pricing (Food Alert Alert65, likely Shield Safety and NSF). Several 2025–2026 UK startups are crowding the low end with AI features.

**Trail (The Access Group)**

Evo adds document storage, custom policy management, APIs/integrations, AI Co-Pilot and SSO — [Trail pricing](https://trailapp.com/pricing)

- Trail + Surefoot bundle: £105/site/month — [Trail Surefoot](https://trailapp.com/surefoot)
- Owned by The Access Group. Access says Trail plugs into its Training, HR/scheduling, EPoS, CRM, reservations, property maintenance and purchasing products via Access Workspace, and claims "ePOS, rotas and 1,500+ apps" integrations — [Access Group acquires Trail](https://www.theaccessgroup.com/en-gb/about/news/the-access-group-acquires-trail/); [Access checklists](https://theaccessgroup.com/en-ie/hospitality/software/checklists)

### Inferences
- The price anchor for self-serve food safety at UK SMEs has dropped to about £19/site/month on rolling contracts (Hubl, Lemon). Trail's £38 entry price on a 12-month minimum and Navitas's £129–159 on 12–24 months are now the "premium" points. A combined app (checklists + food safety + R&M + handbook) could plausibly charge £35–60/site/month if it replaces two or three tools, but it will be compared against £19 food-safety-only apps.
- Few players combine all four pillars. Trail (with Evo policy management and Access's maintenance/HR suite) and the Citation/Food Alert bundle come closest at the enterprise end. CompliChef is the nearest SME all-in-one. R&M (repairs & maintenance) looks like the least-served pillar among UK food-safety apps.

### Gaps
- Not researched within budget: Kitchen Checks (no pricing found), Checkit current pricing, Planday, Deputy, Bizimply, Fourth, Harri and Tanda checklist modules, Shield Safety Group and NSF apps, Ecolab, probe brands (Comark, ETI/Thermapen, Hanna, Cooper-Atkins) and their bundle prices, maintenance tools (Joblogic, Planet FM, MaintainX, Hospitality Maintenance), Planglow, Croner, Citation HR/Atlas pricing, ToolKit/ESS/ShiftWorks, and Companies House financials.
- Navitas £129/£159 comes only from a competitor's blog and needs vendor confirmation.

## Q3. Published UK pricing, terms, setup and hardware; EPOS/rota integrations; review sentiment

### Takeaway
Clear published per-site pricing exists for Trail (£38–£82.50, 12-month minimum), Hubl and Lemon (£19, rolling), SFBB Pro (£25.95 + £12.95 for extra sites, £99.95 printer), SFBB+ (£4.99), Paddl (£69) and Nutritics (£16–£64). Alert65, Navitas (officially), Operandio and enterprise tiers are quote-only. Confirmed named EPOS integrations were scarce in the evidence. Trail/Access claims generic EPoS/rota integrations, and Opsi is a Toast partner. Review data is thin: Trail scores 4.9/5 on Capterra UK, but from only 7 reviews.

### Inferences
- Integration depth with UK EPOS (Zonal, Tevalis, Lightspeed, Epos Now, Square, SumUp, Kobas) is not a visible selling point for the food-safety apps. A new entrant with real EPOS and rota connectors (sales and covers driving checklists, rota driving task assignment and training/handbook acknowledgements) would stand out, although Access and Fourth can bundle this for enterprise.
- Review counts are very low across the category on Capterra UK, so App Store ratings and Trustpilot are likely to matter more for SME buyers.
