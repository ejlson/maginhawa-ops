# Competitive Landscape: Restaurant Operations / Checklist / Food-Safety Compliance Software (as of Oct 2026)

## What exactly is Check65?

### Takeaway
Check65 is the free mobile companion app to **Alert65**, the food-safety and health-and-safety compliance platform from **Food Alert**, a London consultancy founded in 1990. The Citation Group has owned Food Alert since 2019, and the app's publisher is Citation Holdings Limited. It is a UK, consultancy-led product. It sells to independents, multi-site groups and enterprise. The software comes bundled with advisory services such as a 24/7 advice line, audits, training and HACCP development. No public price is published.

### Cited Findings
- The Check65 iOS app is published by **Citation Holdings Limited**. It is free, in the Business category, and requires iOS/iPadOS 15.6+ or an M1+ Mac. It has only 2 US ratings (4.0). Version 1.10.2 added up to 10 attachments per checklist action and in-app Food Alert notifications. One reviewer reported a white screen on login. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/check65/id1611955849)
- The app describes itself as the mobile companion to the Alert65 food hygiene platform. It covers daily, weekly and monthly scheduled checklists (opening/closing, cleaning, fire safety) and ad-hoc checklists (visitor logs, unscheduled cleaning). It also covers temperature checks for hot, cold and delivery items, role-based task filtering, and notifications so no checklist is missed. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/check65/id1611955849); [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.check65&hl=en_GB)
- Alert65 features: digital HACCP plans with templates, a library of 200+ consultant-written risk assessments, site-specific safety policies applied group-wide, an allergen matrix, training tracking, monitoring of statutory certification deadlines, logging of food complaints and incidents, accident investigation, and EHO inspection-readiness tools. — [Food Alert Alert65 page](https://www.foodalert.com/software/alert65/)
- Check65 features include mobile and desktop checklists, a green/amber/red priority score, temperature monitoring integration (Bluetooth temperature sensors), team accountability dashboards and offline mode. — [Food Alert](https://www.foodalert.com/software/alert65/)

  - Pricing is by contact form only

### Inferences
- Check65 is not a standalone SaaS product. It is the field app of a consultancy-plus-software bundle, and its strength is regulatory content (UK EHO and Food Hygiene Rating Scheme readiness). A new entrant is unlikely to beat it on compliance expertise. It is more likely to win on UX, self-serve onboarding, transparent pricing, repairs and maintenance, and handbook features. Check65 has none of these four in its public feature list.
- The very low app-store review count (2 US ratings) suggests a UK-centric installed base that is distributed through enterprise contracts rather than app-store discovery.
- Its parent, Citation, is a large UK HR, health-and-safety and compliance group. That gives Check65 distribution through cross-sell into Citation's HR and employment-law customers, which overlaps with the "employee handbook/policies" use case.

### Gaps
- No public Alert65/Check65 price was found. Pricing is quote-only.
- The year Check65 launched was not found. The iOS app ID (1611955849) suggests roughly 2022, but this is unverified.
- Integrations beyond Bluetooth temperature sensors (POS, HR, SSO) were not found.
- I could not confirm whether any other product named "Check65" exists. Searches returned only the Food Alert/Citation app.

## Who are the main competitors, and what do they offer and cost?

### Takeaway
The market splits into five groups:
1. **US multi-unit ops platforms**: Jolt, Crunchtime Ops (formerly Zenput and Squadle), Xenia, Operandio, Delightree. Most of these are quote-based, roughly $40–$300 per location per month.
2. **Food-safety and HACCP specialists**: FoodDocs, ComplianceMate (now Ladle), Navitas, Food Alert. These carry the HACCP, IoT and compliance depth.
3. **UK checklist apps**: Trail, Culinary Key. These are transparent and per-site, at £32–£82.50 per site per month.
4. **Horizontal tools**: SafetyCulture, Connecteam, MaintainX, UpKeep, FacilityBot. These are priced per user or per hub.
5. **Scheduling and back-office suites that add tasks as an add-on**: 7shifts, Homebase, Restaurant365. Their task add-ons cost about $13 per location per month.

AI-first entrants in 2025–26 focus on voice, cameras and agents (Palona, Owner, Nory) more than on checklists.

## Which features are table stakes and which are differentiators?

### Takeaway
**Table stakes** across nearly all products:
- scheduled and ad-hoc checklists
- temperature logs
- mobile app plus desktop
- multi-location dashboards
- role-based tasks
- photo attachments
- notifications
- unlimited or cheap users

**Current differentiators**:
- IoT wireless sensors (with hardware revenue and cost)
- label printing
- HACCP plan builders and traceability
- auto corrective actions tied to failed checks
- tamper-proof live-capture photos
- native work orders and maintenance
- policy and handbook management
- LMS and training
- regulatory content and consultancy
- AI (co-pilots, AI-generated HACCP and reports, computer vision)

### Cited Findings
- **Bundled checklists and temperature checks are universal**: Check65 bundles checklists, temperature checks and role filtering. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/check65/id1611955849)

## What pricing models do competitors use?

### Takeaway
- **Per location per month**: the dominant model for restaurant-specific tools (Trail, Culinary Key, FoodDocs, Jolt, 7shifts, Delightree).
- **Per user**: horizontal tools (SafetyCulture, MaintainX, UpKeep; Navitas on Capterra).
- **Per hub or per user band**: Connecteam, Xenia.
- **Hardware**: IoT vendors add sensor hardware ($100–$400 per sensor; $2.5k–$4k per site for ComplianceMate).
- **Contracts**: UK players increasingly require 12-month minimums (Trail; Navitas 1–2 years). Culinary Key competes on cancel-anytime terms.

- A reasonable price window for a new US entrant aimed at independents and small groups is about $40–$120 per location per month. That is above the $13 task add-ons and below Jolt and FoodDocs Standard.
- In the UK the window is about £30–£75 per site per month.

### Gaps
- Most enterprise prices (Crunchtime, Jolt, Operandio, Delightree, Alert65, SmartSense) are quote-only. The figures above are third-party estimates and should be treated as indicative.

## Which segments does each competitor target?

### Takeaway
- **Independents**: Culinary Key, Trail Team, FoodDocs Lite, Homebase, 7shifts, Connecteam free tiers, and Owner (AI).
- **Multi-unit chains**: Jolt, Xenia, Trail Standard/Evo, FoodDocs Professional, ComplianceMate/Ladle.
- **Enterprise and franchise**: Crunchtime Ops, Operandio (10+ locations), Delightree, SmartSense, Food Alert enterprise, Palona.

### Cited Findings
- Trail Team covers 1–3 sites, and Standard covers 1 to 1,000+ sites. — [Culinary Key comparison](https://www.culinarykey.co.uk/compare/trail-vs-culinary-key-which-food-safety-app-is-right-for-your-uk-food-business)
- Operandio is optimized for 10+ locations and franchises. — [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/246429/Operandio/)
- Delightree targets franchise systems. — [Delightree](https://www.delightree.com/pricing)
- Jolt is reported to be "steep for smaller businesses". — [Taqtics](https://taqtics.co/reviews/jolt/)
- Xenia's own page says Zenput remains defensible "if you only need checklists, you only run restaurants, and you already pay for Crunchtime back-office". — [Xenia](https://www.xenia.team/vs/xenia-vs-zenput)
- Food Alert serves independents (1–2 sites) up to enterprise. — [Food Alert](https://www.foodalert.com/software/alert65/)

### Inferences
- The independent and small-group segment (1–10 locations) is served either by cheap generic task add-ons or by single-purpose food-safety apps. An affordable all-in-one covering ops, repairs and maintenance, handbooks and food safety, with transparent pricing, looks like the clearest white space.
