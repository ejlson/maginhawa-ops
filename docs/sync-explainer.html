How syncing works between devices and accounts

How the kitchen iPad, managers' phones and head office see the same end-of-day report and checklists, and what happens when two people edit at once.

One shared copy per site and day. Every device keeps its own copy and keeps it in step.

A device saves to itself first, sends changes in the background, and checks for other people's changes. The copy belongs to the site, so accounts only decide who can reach it and whose name goes on each change.

How a change travels

In order
1
Saved on the deviceEvery edit is kept on the device straight away, so a reload or lost signal loses nothing.
2
Sent in the backgroundThe report goes up with the version it started from. Checklist ticks are sent in groups, about once a second.
3
Other devices check inAbout every 10 s while in use, every 30 s when idle, never while hidden, and at once when the app comes back into view or signal returns.
4
Cheap answers when nothing changedThe server answers "no change" from Netlify Blobs without asking SharePoint, which limits how often we can call it.
5
Edits made outside the app still arriveA Blobs entry is only trusted for 60 s, so a change typed straight into the SharePoint list shows up within about a minute.

How accounts fit in

Who can reach what
JS
Same site, different peopleEveryone works on the same report and checklists for that site and day. The server takes your name from your sign-in, never from what the device sends.
iP
Shared kitchen iPadPeople sign in with PINs and switch from the name menu. The iPad's copy belongs to the site and day, so switching person doesn't split the report.
2+
One person, more than one siteEach site keeps its own report and checklists. Switching site opens that site's copy.
HO
Head officeCan open any site's screens. Changes are recorded as "Name (head office)".

PermissionsThe server refuses any site you aren't allowed into. Only supervisors and above can correct a finished checklist.
