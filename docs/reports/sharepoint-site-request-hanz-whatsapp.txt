• Primary admin: me
• Language: *English (UK)* (it can't be changed later)

```https://graph.microsoft.com/v1.0/sites/<site id>/permissions```

```{"roles":["fullcontrol"],"grantedToIdentities":[{"application":{"id":"<client ID>","displayName":"Maginhawa App"}}]}```
