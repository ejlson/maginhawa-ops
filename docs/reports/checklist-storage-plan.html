How checklists are stored in SharePoint

Text, numbers and photos for each site's day, saved by our own Netlify Functions through Microsoft Graph. You can get, add, change and delete any checklist, and every day a site misses is recorded. All figures are estimates or examples, not real data.

Every checklist is found by one key
BUN|2026-11-06

Site code + trading date. Each active site gets exactly one day record per date, whether or not it submits. Runs, answers and photos all hang off this key, so the app, offline devices, the nightly jobs and Excel can find a day without knowing any SharePoint id.

~2,920day records a year (8 sites × 365)
~410kanswer rows a year, split by year
~9 GBphotos a year at ~300 KB each

What a day looks like

One day record, the checklists done that day (opening, closing, temperature checks), one row per final answer, and photos as files.

DAY
ChecklistDays · BUN|2026-11-06
Status: Due, In progress, Submitted, Late, Missed, Not trading, Deleted. Counts, who submitted, a hash of the day.
1 per site per day
RUN
ChecklistRuns · BUN|2026-11-06|closing|22:30
One per checklist and due time. While open, its answers live as a JSON snapshot on this item, saved with If-Match.
5–10 a day
ANS
Responses_2026 · …|walkin-temp|0
Written once at Finish: typed value (number, text, yes/no, date), Pass/Fail, who, when. Corrections add rev 1, 2… and never edit rev 0.
20–80 per run
IMG
Photos library · BUN/2026/11/{PhotoId}.jpg
Write-once JPEG named by an id made on the device. Columns tie it to the day, run and question. Answers store the id, never a URL.
~80 a day

A checklist's day, end to end

  1. 05:00 · day record createdThe morning job writes BUN|2026-11-06 as Due, or Not trading. Re-running it is harmless because the key is unique.
  2. On the iPad · answers saved on the device firstEach answer goes into the device queue at once, so no signal means no lost work. Every 10–20 s one batch is sent. Photos are compressed, stripped of location data, hashed and uploaded on their own.
  3. Server · rules applied onceChecks the person's sites, loads the run by key, applies last-write-wins per answer, saves with If-Match and returns the new version. Other devices see it on their next poll.
  4. Finish · checklist lockedRefused if someone answered since the finisher last looked. Who and when are stamped by the server, and the run is hashed.
  5. Background · answer rows writtenFinal answers are copied to Responses_2026, paced under Graph's limit, then the run is marked Written.
  6. Close time · gaps recordedUnfinished due checklists become Missed. A late finish turns the day Late, but the missed time stays on record.
  7. Later · found, corrected or deletedHead office finds the day by key or date range, corrects it with a reason, soft-deletes or restores it, or hard-deletes it.

Five fixes to the current spec

1
Text date keys, not Date columns
SharePoint stores a date as midnight London time, so in summer Graph returns the day before. Add BusinessDateKey (2026-11-06).
Wrong dates
2
Text keys, not lookups
An offline device can't know a SharePoint item id, and lookups count towards a 12-per-query limit.
Offline
3
Answers list: indexes first, one list per year
SharePoint stops building indexes after about 20,000 items. At about 1,250 a day that's day 16.
Day 16
4
Store photo ids, not links
Download links expire in about an hour. Hyperlink columns are awkward to write through Graph.
Broken links
5
Lock records without E5
Record labels need E5 and can't be set on list items through Graph. Use: app-only writes, read-only people, versioning, hashes and a site retention policy.
Licence

Decisions for you

Name the day listRename DayClosures to ChecklistDays (recommended), or keep the old name.
Hard deletesOne head-office person with a confirm screen, or two people (request + approve). Two is safer.
How long to keep photos2 years like the checks, or about 12 months unless tied to an incident. No legal minimum; confirm with the EHO.
LicencesCheck the tenant has E3 or Business Premium, which the retention policy needs.
