Maginhawa Ops · redesign direction · November 2026

Every site has its own colour. Every screen says what's next.

We keep what works (one main card per screen, clean lists, progress circles) and add three things: a colour that tells you which site you're in, a clear "up next" through the day, and search on every screen.

{{s.mono}}
Know where you are
The site's colour and initials sit at the top of every screen and colour the main card. Switching site changes the colour in a quarter of a second, so you feel the move.
Know what's next
Today reads top to bottom: Needs you, Now, Later today. The main card always holds one "Up next" button. The close has a bar that says what's left and jumps you there.
Find anything
Four tabs on the phone (Today, Close, Checks, More) and search at the top of every screen. It finds sites, past days ("Belly 3 Nov"), checklists, people and settings.

How colour is used
KT
Site colour = where you are. Site badge, the slowly moving gradient on the main card (a blend of the site’s colours, with grain), the selected tab, progress circles.
Green, amber and red = how it's going. They never change with the site and always come with words: "£20.00 short", "Missed".
Aa
Text colour is worked out for you. Light or dark text on each site colour, checked for contrast in light and dark mode.
Never on body text, status pills or whole page backgrounds. Never colour alone: the initials always go with it.
