What we borrowed, and why
Patterns from apps people already find easy, picked for managers closing late on a phone and head office checking 8 sites at once.
Site colour
LINEAR · RADIXOne colour per site; the rest is worked outEach site stores one colour. Lighter and deeper shades and the gradient blend come from it, so custom colours always look right. Source
ARC · SLACKThe whole view changes when you change placeSwitching site fades the badge, card and tab tint together in about a quarter of a second. Source
TODOIST · GOOGLE CALENDARTested swatches first, custom second12 swatches checked in light and dark. Custom picks a shade, and we pick the text colour so it's always readable. Source
APPLE HIG · MONZONever colour aloneSite colour always comes with its initials, and green, amber and red only ever mean status. Source
Finding things
7SHIFTS · AIRBNB HOSTNeeds you → Now → Later todayToday opens on what needs action, then the day in order, with exact counts ("1 late · 1 failed", not "2 items"). Source
LINEAR MOBILEFour tabs and search everywhereToday, Close, Checks, More. Temperatures or Repairs can be pinned. Search finds sites, past days, checks, people and settings. Source
TRAILOne card per site, with a "needs a look" filterHead office see all 8 sites at once: net, wages and status in words, filtered to what needs attention. Source
THINGS 3Group the day by timeChecks sit under Opening, Through the day and Closing, so the list reads like the shift. Source
Motion
APPLE · EMIL KOWALSKIFast, interruptible, never in the wayPress 0.97 in 120ms, screens under 300ms, never grow from zero, no animation on things used dozens of times a shift. Source
FAMILYScreens open from what you tappedSections open from their row like a tray, so you never lose your place. Delight is saved for "Day sent". Source
NUMBERFLOWFigures roll as you typeNet and wages slide digit by digit, so a change is easy to notice. Source
MATERIAL 3Leave faster than you arriveSheets come in over 360ms and go in 220ms. Reduce motion swaps movement for a short fade. Source
Easy to follow
THINGS 3A progress circle on every section and checklistDone, half done or to do can be seen at a glance, in the site's colour.
OUR RESEARCHA "what's left" barThe close always says what's left and jumps you to the next unfinished section.
TRAILFix a failed check in the rowA failed fridge reading offers "Moved food", "Rechecked" or "Called repair" right there, with no extra page. Source
FIRE GRADIENTS (YOUR REFERENCE)Soft colour, gentle movement, a little grainEach site's card blends its own colours and drifts slowly. Text sits on the deepest part so it stays readable.
We avoid
Colour pickers with no contrast check Site colour on body text or status pills Counts that mix different things Bouncy or slow animation on everyday taps Confetti on routine ticks More than 5 tabs, or Temperatures over 2 taps deep
