Maginhawa Ops · first-run tour

A one-minute tour, made for each role

Everyone sees a short welcome the first time they sign in, then a few tips that light up the real controls. Staff, supervisors, managers and head office each get only their own tips. All names and figures are examples.

How it works

6 rules
Once per personShown at first sign-in, on any device. On the shared iPad, once for each PIN.
Short4 or 5 tips, one sentence each. About a minute.
Points at the real thingEach tip lights up a live control and dims the rest. You can tap the control itself.
Never in the waySkip at any time, and Undo brings it back. If a close is due within the hour, it offers Later first.
Tips on new screensThe first time someone opens cash count, send back or a new checklist, one tip shows. "Got it" hides it for good.
Works for everyoneFocus moves to the tip and Esc closes it. Screen readers hear "Tip 2 of 5". With reduced motion on, nothing slides.
